S.P.L.O.O.G.E

Two drone flights over two sites, six days apart, rebuilt as a single photorealistic 3D reconstruction and then flown back through in a browser alongside the footage they came from. COLMAP for the camera solve, a Rust and WebGPU trainer for the Gaussians, WebGL for playback. The write-up is laid out as a paper, because the ground turning see-through turned out to be one line in the renderer rather than the training failure I spent a week measuring.

P.E.T.E.R

One command goes and finds a TV clip, figures out which episode it's from, traces it to line art, rebuilds it as parametric expressions inside a Desmos graphing calculator, screenshots it frame by frame, and posts the finished video. About 4,100 lines of Python, no framework. The write-up has the tracer and the expression trick as things you can actually play with.
