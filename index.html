S.P.L.O.O.G.E

Two drone flights over two spots, six days apart, rebuilt into one photorealistic 3D reconstruction you can then fly back through in a browser, next to the footage it came from. COLMAP for the camera solve, a Rust and WebGPU trainer for the Gaussians, WebGL for playback. The write-up is laid out like a paper. The ground going see-through turned out to be one line in the renderer, after I'd spent a week measuring it as a training problem.

P.E.T.E.R

One command goes and finds a TV clip, works out which episode it's from, traces it to line art, rebuilds it as parametric expressions inside a Desmos graphing calculator, screenshots it frame by frame and posts the finished video. About 4,100 lines of Python, no framework. In the write-up you can push the tracer and the expression trick around yourself. There's a colour version that runs inside Excel too.

S.H.R.E.D

Cuts debate evidence cards with a language model running on my laptop. Give it a claim and it finds sources that argue it, then marks up each card by picking word indices, so it can't misquote anything. I scored it against 970 cards my team cut by hand, then re-tuned it when the people reading the cards disagreed with the score.
