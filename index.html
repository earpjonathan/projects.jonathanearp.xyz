Desmos Guy

One command goes and finds a TV clip, figures out which episode it's from, traces it to line art, rebuilds it as parametric expressions inside a Desmos graphing calculator, screenshots it frame by frame, and posts the finished video. About 4,100 lines of Python, no framework. The write-up has the tracer and the expression trick as things you can actually play with.
